# Third-party component credits

The `visual-v2.css` and `visual-v2.js` enhancements adapt the interaction
patterns of **Spotlight** and **Animated Border** from
[Kevin Girelli's Corsair UI](https://github.com/KevinGirelli/corsair-ui),
published in the [21st.dev](https://21st.dev/@kevingirelli/components/spotlight)
community library. The icon-specific animations are adapted from the movement
patterns of [lucide-animated](https://github.com/pqoqubbw/icons). Both sources
are released under the MIT License. The GEX site keeps its own SVG geometry,
HTML, CSS, and lightweight event handlers so it does not need React at runtime.
